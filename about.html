Our Story

A Legacy of Quality

Founded in 2000, ACONCN has grown from a small manufacturing workshop into a globally recognized leader in composite manhole cover production. Our journey is defined by continuous innovation, unwavering quality standards, and a commitment to customer satisfaction.

Located in China's industrial heartland, our state-of-the-art facility spans over 20,000 square meters and houses advanced SMC/BMC compression molding production lines. Every product leaving our factory undergoes rigorous testing to ensure compliance with international standards including EN 124, SGS, and ISO 9001.

Today, ACONCN products are installed in infrastructure projects across 50+ countries, serving municipalities, construction companies, and industrial facilities worldwide. Our team of 200+ skilled professionals works tirelessly to deliver solutions that stand the test of time.
